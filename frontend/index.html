Why Vizag?

Tourism Highlights

A harmonious blend of natural wonders, maritime valor, and spiritual tranquility.

🏖️

Blue Flag Clean Coast

Home to Rushikonda Beach, certified with the prestigious international Blue Flag for clean coastal waters, safety facilities, and exciting water sports.

🚢

Naval Maritime Trio

The only shoreline in Asia where you can step inside a decommissioned submarine (INS Kursura), an aircraft museum (TU-142), and a Sea Harrier jump jet fighter.

⛰️

Araku & Borra Caves

Ride the glass-roof Vistadome train into the Eastern Ghats through 84 tunnels, arriving at million-year-old stalactite caves and fragrant organic coffee plantations.

About The City

The City of Destiny

Visakhapatnam, colloquially known as Vizag, is the largest city and commercial capital of Andhra Pradesh. Nestled gracefully between the Eastern Ghats and the Bay of Bengal, the city boasts a rich maritime history dating back to the 6th century BCE when it was part of the Kalinga Kingdom.

With natural deepwater harbours, Buddhist monastic settlements at Thotlakonda and Bojjannakonda, the ancient Simhachalam hilltop temple, and untouched coastal landscapes, Visakhapatnam welcomes visitors with warmth, rich Andhra culinary delights, and unforgettable landscapes.
